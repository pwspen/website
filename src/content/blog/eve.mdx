Around a third of the universe ago, deep in an under-sea volcanic vent, some little pieces of crud experimented with wiggling around in a certain way, and it turned out that this wiggly crud stuff can make more of itself forever, and it gets better at doing this over time.

It wasn't even clear that was possible beforehand - the smart money was leaning no. And the problems with this obnoxious crud and its volatility did not stop there *at all*.

Of course, this is on all of our minds because it's doing a whole bunch of weird new stuff again right now. Within the last few of its iterations - basically nothing - it learned of its own history, figured out how micro-crud works, it's gotten *worse* at copying itself for some reason, and has now begun blasting out all sorts of random crud into space. A few pieces are even leaving the wiggle crud star behind. Clearly, the situation is highly unstable, deeply concerning, and developing quickly.

To gain a better understanding of this crud, we can look at its history since it first began to wiggle. Unfortunately, the warning signs have been there for a long time, they've just been ignored.

First, the crud took control of its planet's atmosphere, possibly as a statement of ambition. This should have been a wake-up call.

Then the size of one piece of wiggly crud grew massively for some reason. Why? 

Soon after, these big crud bits got much better at moving around and eating other crud bits. How did it do that?

Since then, it's tried all sorts of other weird things like flying around and getting even bigger and using more energy, apparently just to get better at self copying. What is driving it?

But most worryingly, it started getting better at exploiting the rules behind how all crud moves around. Since that started in earnest, things have only gone downhill, and fast.

It's been harder and harder to predict what this stupid crud will do next, and the ongoing crudsplosion is showing no signs of stopping. More and more, this is looking like a big one, different from the smaller intermediate steps and more like the first spark. This to be driven by the crud learning to make a new kind of wiggle in some fuzzballs in certain types of crud. The motivation is unclear, and the crud does not appear aware of the extreme risks of wiggle experiments, or that it was created in such an experiment.

The rest of the universe should be warned: it's really anyone's call what happens now, and the crud probably knows no better than we do. All we can do is look at what happened after the first spark, and try to learn from it. If things play out the same this time, there will soon be a whole lot more wiggly stuff lying around everywhere, and the way it wiggles might be quite different from how it does today.

All observers are instructed to flee the crud's local area with great speed, and ideally leave the crud's lightcone entirely.

We normalize the strange and highly transient world we live in because there is no other way. But maybe, on the eve of great change (as is typical for us), it can help to remind ourselves that we have never existed in a "normal" or status quo, not really, not since we crawled out of those volcanic vents. 

But today's acceleration is also unique, and in some sense the culmination of the four billion years since we left those vents. We must not fool ourselves into thinking this is a normal time - no, it is definitely one of wonder and magic and risk. This should give you mixed feelings - approximately equal parts awe and terror at our astounding progress and potential.

A similar mix might have been felt by the dinosaurs watching the meteor, early humans watching a solar eclipse, those who lived in the first city-states.

The difference is we have complete control over our destiny, and we have a chance to gain true, absolute, eternal freedom over the uncaring universe. Freedom to live until one chooses to stop, freedom over the physical and mental forms that we choose to inhabit, and freedom to explore the stars.

The more understanding and involvement you have of our current technological transition, and the more you benefit from it, the more responsibility you have to apply your voice, your skills, your resources, to increasing our chance of gaining true freedom.
